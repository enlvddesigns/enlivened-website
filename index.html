How it works

From request to delivery.

The same simple path either way.

Step 1

Apply or request

Creatives share their skills. Organizations submit a brief.

Step 2

We match

We pair the brief with a volunteer whose skills fit it.

Step 3

The work gets made

Creative and organization work together directly.

Step 4

Deliver and grow

The org gets human-made work. The creative gets a portfolio piece.

From the founder

Angel Gupta

I've been editing for the past 5 years. I don't know every tool out there, and I'm not perfect. But I have a vision for this, and I'm learning every step of the way, alongside the volunteers I meet from every creative field.

Creativity is more than a finished product. It's the process of experimenting, communicating, and bringing a perspective nothing else could.

"We value the imagination and human experience that inspire original ideas, and built ENLVD to keep that in the work."
What we stand for

The practice behind the work.

Made by someone

Every deliverable traces back to a person who made choices, not a model.

Collective over individual

Skills are shared, not hoarded. Newer creatives learn from ones who've been doing this longer.

Access over exclusivity

Small businesses and NGOs get the same care a big client pays for, at no cost.

Process is the point

We care as much about a creative's growth through a project as we do about the final file.

Real deadlines

Volunteering doesn't mean casual. Every brief gets a timeline and gets delivered.

Room to evolve

Start as a beginner exploring a craft, leave with a portfolio and a network that keeps opening doors.
